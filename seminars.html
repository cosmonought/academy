Policies

These apply across all Academy seminars unless a specific seminar states otherwise.

Registration

Registering submits your interest with your name, email, X handle, and a brief note on why you'd like to join. Registration alone does not grant access.

Enrollment

To complete enrollment, DM @NetaDAO_Academy on X from your registered X handle. Once enrolled, you'll have access to readings, the Cinema screening, and speaking access during the seminar's live X Spaces discussions.

Participation

Participants are expected to engage thoughtfully with each session's materials. Specific participation requirements (for example, proof-of-participation tokens) may vary by seminar and will be noted on that seminar's own page.

Non-Attribution

Some seminars address sensitive or disturbing material. To encourage candid study, we observe a policy of non-attribution: you may publicly discuss themes and arguments raised in a session, but should not attribute them to another participant. Learning must include margins for uncertainty, indelicacy, mistake, and dissent.

X Spaces

Speaking access during the live X Spaces discussion is reserved for enrolled participants. At the host's discretion, the discussion may open to general audience Q&A near the end.
