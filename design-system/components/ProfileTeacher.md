# ProfileTeacher (draft)

The profile's **Teacher** tab, for instructors and teaching assistants only; it sits between Student and Account. One seminar at a time (a tab per seminar taught, with the role beside it), a one-line summary, then the seminar's three areas side by side, numbered like the other states, on `rule-strong` dividers:

1. **Enrollment**: requests waiting on the instructor (name, email and X handle in mono, why they're joining, Enroll), then the enrolled participants, each folding open to their details and Unenroll. Unenrolling keeps their history.
2. **Attendance**: the current session's events (lectures and screenings) in date order, each with how many are marked; the one to mark now is open, with a ✓ / — / ? switch per person and Mark all attended. Below it, the log: everyone's record across past sessions, a filled square for attended, an empty one for absent, a dotted one for unrecorded.
3. **Evaluations**: open requests as small forms (state, outcome, agreed form, written feedback, Save); completed ones fold to a line. Where a seminar doesn't offer evaluation the area says so. (Sex, and/or Love doesn't offer it today; the area is filled here to show it.)

A teaching assistant sees Attendance, and the enrolled names in Enrollment without contact details or actions; Evaluations stays with the instructor. Below 1200px the areas stack. Placeholder names and example.com addresses only. Draft for review.
