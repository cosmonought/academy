# CoiningReasonPage (draft)

`/CoiningReason/`: the flagship discussion series. It keeps the seminar page's shell and cover (the copy on the left, the frontispiece filling the right and fading toward the text) and adds what a series of units needs:

- **Cover**: Flagship series, its state (On hiatus, in the neutral badge: nothing is happening now, so no pink), the title fitted to the copy column, the series' own description, and Request archive access.
- **Facts**: when it was held, the units planned and held, where the discussions happen, and the archive on GitHub.
- **Up next**: the unit's next session, 2.12 Money is Sad Shit, in a panel like Sex, and/or Love's Up next, but neutral (an ink rule and an ink-mid kicker, "Up next · On hiatus"): the unit is paused, and pink is for what is happening now.
- **Unit switcher**: Current unit · Archive · Roadmap, as three large tabs on the row's rule; the current one is underlined in ink. Archive shows Unit I in the same layout as Unit II below.
- **The unit**: its name in the display face, its summary and dates, then its sessions as SessionRows (2.0 … 2.11, each folding open to its readings and the recording). A `rule-strong` line marks where the unit paused; the sessions scheduled after it carry the neutral On hiatus badge.
- **Roadmap**: the ten units as tiles, five to a row on hairlines, each with its state and size. The open one is underlined in ink and its summary and topics open below the grid.
- **Archive access**: the three steps. Requesting access opens a unit's materials; it doesn't enroll anyone in a live seminar.

Reading links read "Read ↗" rather than the site's "Click Here ↗". Draft for review.
