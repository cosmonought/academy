Academy seminar policies

Applies to every seminar · review before enrolling

Policy version 2026-09-29-r2

These apply across all Academy seminars unless a specific seminar states otherwise. Expressing interest in a forthcoming seminar is not enrollment and does not create an Academy account.

Registration

When enrollment is open, create an account with your email and a password, then request enrollment with your name, X handle, and a brief note on why you'd like to join. Creating an account lets you sign in; seminar access follows enrollment confirmation.

Enrollment

Enrollment requires explicit agreement to the current Academy Seminar Policies. To complete enrollment, DM @NetaDAO_Academy on X from your registered X handle. Once enrolled, you'll have access to readings, the Cinema screening, and speaking access during the seminar's live X Spaces discussions.

Participation

Participants are expected to engage thoughtfully with each session's materials. Specific participation requirements (for example, proof-of-participation tokens) may vary by seminar and will be noted on that seminar's own page.

Attendance

Enrollment assumes an intention to participate. A participant who does not attend any sessions, or who ceases participating without notice, may be disenrolled at the instructor's discretion.

Conduct

Participants are expected to engage with one another, instructors, and seminar materials in good faith and with respect. Harassment, deliberate disruption, or persistently bad-faith participation may result in disenrollment.

Non-Attribution

Some seminars address sensitive or disturbing material. To encourage candid study, we observe a policy of non-attribution: you may publicly discuss themes and arguments raised in a session, but should not attribute them to another participant. Learning must include margins for uncertainty, indelicacy, mistake, and dissent.

X Spaces

Speaking access during the live X Spaces discussion is reserved for enrolled participants. At the host's discretion, the discussion may open to general audience Q&A near the end.
