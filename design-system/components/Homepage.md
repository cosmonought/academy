# Homepage

The homepage: the statement across the page, one row with each real program once, and the footer. Built from the mockup and cut down to what the Academy runs today.

## Structure
- SiteHeader, sticky, without its wordmark until the statement's first line has scrolled under it (`data-nda-dock`): on the homepage that line is the masthead.
- HeroStatement across the page (`nda-hero--page`) at the intro's scale, never taller than the first screen allows; THOUGHT carries the pigment film. Under it the tag line, "Ideas · Community · Discipline · For a more open tomorrow", fading in one tag at a time (`data-nda-sequence`).
- One row of four ProgramPanels, type only, current first, each index rule naming its state: Sex, and/or Love (Active, with **Late enroll**), Coining Reason (On hiatus; its page holds the archive), Sex, Monsters, and Superheroes (Forthcoming, with **Express interest**), and Fork, the only Fork on the page, titled with its mark over "The Journal" on one line, with **Explore the journal** (a CircleLink) at its foot.
- SiteFooter.

## Decisions since the mockup
- Display type is NDA Display (Archivo, cut condensed), set tight with open word spaces like the intro film: Bold for every line, Heavy for THOUGHT, which is drawn 1.6 times as tall.
- The statement spans the page, so the intro's last frame and the homepage are the same picture.
- No Seminar access tiles and no journal blocks: with one seminar in each state, they repeated the panels. AccessTile and JournalPanel stay in the system for later.
- Only THOUGHT carries the pigment film. Program titles are solid `ink`; panels fade in the moving wash on hover and focus.
- No artwork on the homepage: THOUGHT's film is its one image, and the moving wash is the hover's. Each seminar's artwork lives on its own page (the cover) and on the seminars index.
- The header links to the site's real destinations: Seminars, Cinema, Journal, Sign in. The footer adds only X, Discord and Neta DAO.
- Fork appears as its mark, whole until hovered, and has no wash until fork.netadao.org has its own identity.
