# ProfileTeacher (draft)

The profile's **Teacher** tab, for instructors and teaching assistants only; it sits between Student and Account. One seminar at a time (a tab per seminar taught, with the role beside it), a one-line summary, then the seminar's three areas, numbered like the other states, on `rule-strong` dividers:

1. **Attendance**, full width: one table. Participants down the left (sticky as it scrolls), every meeting across the top in order: the session and kind in mono (the kind's parts never break apart), the title in the display face, the date. The table scrolls sideways and opens on the **current meeting**, as far left as it goes (near the end of the table, on the whole column before it): the first meeting that hasn't been over for a day, a screening being over four hours after it starts, so it moves on 24 hours after each one. That column carries the pink rule and a faint tint, with Next (or Now, once it has begun) by its date. A second header row lines up each meeting's tally and All present. Each cell is one box, present or absent: no third state.
2. **Enrollment**: requests waiting on the instructor (name, email and X handle in mono, why they're joining, Enroll), then the enrolled participants, one line each with **Unenroll on the row**; the name folds open to the email, handle and reason. Unenroll asks first and keeps their history.
3. **Evaluations**: open requests as small forms (state, outcome, agreed form, written feedback, Save); completed ones fold to a line. Where a seminar doesn't offer evaluation the area says so. (Sex, and/or Love doesn't offer it today; the area is filled here to show it.)

A teaching assistant sees Attendance, and the enrolled names without contact details or actions; Evaluations stays with the instructor. For a teaching assistant a screening goes by its **session's title** until the syllabus reveals the film (when the screening begins), so the table never names a film early; the draft shows the two later screenings that way. On the site, requests sit above the table when there are any. Below 1200px Enrollment and Evaluations stack. Placeholder names and example.com addresses only. Draft for review.
