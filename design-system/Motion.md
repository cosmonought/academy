# Motion

Three things move: the pigment inside THOUGHT, the hover state of a panel (the wash fading in and the artwork turning to colour, or the Fork mark being cut), and the first-visit intro. Everything else changes state in `dur-fast` (150ms) or not at all.

## THOUGHT: the pigment loop

- Source: the pigment film (10 s, pink and blue paint over black). The loop is its 2.5–10 s section cut into a 6-second seamless loop with a 1.5 s crossfade, so it never jumps back to black.
- Two cuts, each as WebM (VP9, listed first) and MP4 (H.264):
  - `pigment-loop-ink` for Paper: the paint over black, highlights held below `paper` so the `lighten` key leaves the page clean. Black pockets in the paint read as ink inside the letters.
  - `pigment-loop-cream` for Night: the black keyed to the cream `ink`, shadows lifted above the black ground, keyed with `darken`.
- Posters (`pigment-poster-ink.webp`, `pigment-poster-cream.webp`) are each loop's first frame; they show before the film loads, without JavaScript, and under reduced motion.
- `NDA.liquidWord()` plays the film only while the word is on screen and the tab is visible, swaps cuts when the theme changes, and never plays under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- THOUGHT is drawn 1.6 times its natural height (`--nda-word-stretch`), in the hero as in the intro.
- On the site pages, only THOUGHT. No other word, title or panel gets the film.

## The pigment wash

- Hover or keyboard focus on a ProgramPanel or AccessTile fades in the wash over `dur-wash` with `ease-out`, and fades it out the same way. A program's artwork goes from greyscale to colour on the same timing.
- The wash moves: it is the THOUGHT loop blurred into a soft field (the black filled in from the paint around it) at 40% strength, slowed to half speed, so the paint drifts behind the panel rather than flowing. It plays only while the panel is hovered or focused and pauses once it has faded out. Two cuts keyed by blending: `pigment-wash-paper` (over white, `multiply`) and `pigment-wash-night` (over black, `screen`). `NDA.wash()` adds the film on a panel's first hover; `pigment-wash.webp`, a still of the same field, shows underneath until the film has a frame, and is the whole wash under reduced motion.
- Adjacent panels show different parts of the wash (`--nda-wash-x` steps 0%, 50%, 100%), so a row reads as one wash moving across it.
- Contrast holds in every frame: `ink` on the wash is at least 9:1 on Paper and 8:1 on Night (the film's darkest and brightest moments are limited frame by frame); the `focus` ring stays above 3:1.
- Coining Reason's panel: the guilloche band fades in over `dur-wash` and flexes as one body while the panel is hovered or focused, a woven tube rather than loose lines: a wave travels along it, one bend per tile in 8s, carrying all 36 curves together, and the mesh swells and narrows about its own midline a quarter-wave behind. The wave grows in over the first second, so the band starts as the still. Drawn on a canvas (`NDA.engrave`); the still band under reduced motion or without script.
- The Graphic's panel: an unseen magnifying glass, a long strip, is drawn across the green Ben-Day dots diagonally, from the top-right corner to the bottom-left (7.5s a pass). Nothing marks the glass; the dots under it are the panel's own seen through it, about three times their size across its middle, the magnification easing off towards its edges, where the enlarged rows bulge, crowd and shrink a little before meeting the page's dots, so it has no edge. As it passes they turn into six-armed asterisks, each dot shrinking as its arms grow, so the change can be watched; behind it they stay asterisks, and the next pass turns them back, and so on while the panel is hovered or focused. Drawn on a canvas (`NDA.comic`); still dots under reduced motion.
- Fork's panels take Fork's own colours instead of the wash: the panel's ground fades to Fork's ice blue-grey (`#E3EAF0`, over `dur-wash`) behind its type, which keeps the Academy's ink, and the ForkMark, whole at rest, is cut: the slash draws left to right (340ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.25,1)`), and 70ms in the lower half slips 6 units right and 5 down with a slight overshoot (460ms, `cubic-bezier(.3,1.4,.5,1)`). Leaving closes it (300ms) and fades the slash (200ms). On touch screens it cuts once when it comes into view.

## The intro (first visit)

A live rebuild of the intro concept film on the Night theme, laid out from measurements of the film: the statement spans 87.5% of a 16:9 screen, THOUGHT's capitals are 29.7% of its width, PHILOSOPHY's 17.3%, and "the home of Web3 and" is fitted to the full width, its capitals about 84% of the first line's (the intro drops the homepage's "is", as the film does). "Neta DAO Academy" never moves. Everything else happens in a window that runs from just under the first line to the screen's bottom edge, so rows rise from the edge.

- **The film in the letters.** `intro-fill` runs under the type from the first frame to the last (the window is a `lighten` group: black ground, cream type, the film in `darken`). It is the cream kinetic film cut for the intro, at 0.83× speed with its jump back already made, so it plays straight through on the clock: a seek mid-sequence would stall the paint in the letters while the browser fetched and decoded the new place, but as in the film the type is mostly cream: the pigment is a stain, not a flood. It flows across PHILOSOPHY and the rows under it at the start (PSYCHOANALYSIS takes it over) and drains away just before the burst: for this the film opens 3.8 s in, where the paint already runs across the frame in streams, and jumps back into step with the score while it is hidden after the burst. From the burst to the reveal the rows are pure cream, but for the ribbon's paint inside a few letters (5.6–6.7 s, from the ribbon's own frames); at the reveal it comes up in a band through THOUGHT, rising from the foot of its first letters to the top of its last, so the top of THOU and the foot of GHT stay cream. It is done with a mask on the film (a soft ellipse that moves, for the stain, and a soft band through THOUGHT, each with its own strength, through registered custom properties); while none of it shows, the film isn't drawn. Without registered properties the film's opacity does the same, coarsely.
- **Type.** The disciplines are set in the Archivo variable font, each at its own width and weight, measured from the film (its rows are heavier, a little wider and more loosely spaced than the statement; CIVICS is far heavier and wider), with normal tracking.
- **The stack.** The rows share one box, from just under the statement to THOUGHT's baseline, with even 1.5cqi gaps, as the film's rows always fill the same space. Each word has its own size (its capitals from 7.2 to 12.4% of the width, before the box's fit): in each set of four the biggest is about two-thirds taller than the smallest (LAW and ECONOMICS big, ART HISTORY and PERFORMANCE STUDIES small; RELIGION big, VISUAL STUDIES small; ETHICS · DESIGN the biggest), and a settled set just fills the box. So a word can only grow by taking room from the others: when a bigger word lands, the rest squash and shift; when a smaller one does, they stretch. Rows are drawn taller or shorter than their natural size to fit (a vertical squash and stretch, never a change of width), with a little overshoot. A long word may run 5cqi past the column's right edge, as PERFORMANCE STUDIES does in the film.
- **Coming and going.** A new row at the bottom (ECONOMICS, HISTORY OF TECHNOLOGY, ECOLOGY) grows in, squashed flat, from the box's floor and pushes the others back up; POLITICS is squashed out and the rows above stretch down into its room before HISTORY OF TECHNOLOGY takes it. Rows that leave at the top scroll up under the statement.
- **Tugs.** Two neighbours fight over the line between them: it swings down and back a few times and settles, the upper row stretching from its top while the lower gives way from its baseline (GOVERNANCE and SPIRITUALITY; ETHICS · DESIGN and CIVICS; SOCIAL THEORY and ECOLOGY).
- **The second line.** It shivers where PHILOSOPHY is squashed up into it (1.45 s). At the burst, ART HISTORY rises into its place and pushes it up; it slips away just under the first line, held there by its own clip, and four rows share the room under the first line until the end. As THOUGHT comes in, it comes back: it drops into place unseen and wipes in from the left, letter by letter, over the top row.
- **The burst.** At 3.2 s, drawn in code as the film has it: a plum spot of light about half the screen across blooms on the wall behind the rows in under a second (plum at the heart, `rgb(120 42 84)`, falling off to the ground), and paint is thrown out of it. Everything leaves the heart together, on the same curve, in 0.6–0.9 s. Four small splashes of thin paint (three blue, one magenta: uneven blots with torn edges, darker where the paint pools) land around the type and keep spreading a little while they hang. Flat shards fly out tumbling, blurred while they fly: pink splinters, blue chips, and shards of the pigment itself, blue, violet and magenta folded together, the paint still moving inside them; one large one, low on the left, turns slowly. Sixteen fly straight on out of the frame; twenty-six hang in the air, drifting and turning slowly, with a scatter of grit. All of it hangs in front of the wall and behind the type, so it shows between the letters, and the spot lights it: plum near its heart, the room's dim, cool light away from it (the paint is multiplied by the light that reaches it, across each splash from the side facing the heart). The splashes and shards cast soft shadows on the wall, pushed out from the spot's heart, further and softer the nearer a piece hangs to us; outside the spot there is no light to cast them. It fades with the spot as THOUGHT comes in. The camera kicks once.
- **The moves.** Unevenly paced vertical scrolls (each row at its own speed and easing, with a trail of motion blur and letters that lag behind the word), page-like peels, twists, rolls (a row rolls up like a drum and the next rolls into its place), italic leans, three shivers (each turns one word into the next), a blur from one word into the next, and a pinch: a compression that travels through HISTORY OF TECHNOLOGY, the letters it reaches shrinking toward the baseline and blurring. Every move ripples the letters slightly through a turbulence displacement that boils at 12 fps.
- **The opening still.** Before anything moves, the first frame stands for 1 s (`HOLD`): the statement over PHILOSOPHY, only the paint moving in PHILOSOPHY's letters, so the eye can take it in before it starts to come apart. The film opens on the motion; the clock includes the still, and every time in the score below is after it.
- **Pacing.** Timed against the film: after the still it starts already moving (HISTORY OF SCIENCE sliding up under PHILOSOPHY) and holds where the film holds (0.35–1.2 s, 2.2–2.75 s, the beat after the cascade, and 6.95–7.8 s, when the rows only tug at each other), so the busy stretches are short bursts between stills.
- **Breathing.** The whole frame drifts out and up while the rows run and comes home for THOUGHT, with a slow 2.35 s breath on top and a fine grain over everything.
- **The opening push.** As in the film: at 1.4 s SOCIOLOGY comes up from the bottom edge and pushes the stack, HISTORY OF SCIENCE (leaning into italic) rises into PHILOSOPHY's place, and PHILOSOPHY is squashed up under the second line to a sliver that hangs there until about 2 s. One timing and easing for all three, so no row ever crosses another. As it lands, HISTORY OF SCIENCE turns into PSYCHOANALYSIS (a quick doubled crossfade), which heads the list; LITERARY THEORY comes up from the edge behind it and the rows close up under the second line as the sliver goes.
- **THOUGHT is peeled in.** As in the film, a right-angled edge, like _|, uncovers it: everything below its horizontal edge and right of its vertical edge stays hidden, and its corner travels from the top of THOU to the lower right, so the tops of T, H, O and U come first, then more of each letter, G and H, the last T last. It runs fast at first and then slower and slower (the film's curve, drawn out by about 1.4 so the peel can be felt), 7.95–8.6 s; the horizontal edge runs a little ahead, so it has cleared the letters' feet when the last T comes through. Each letter lies folded back about a nearly upright hinge and unfolds flat as the edge crosses it, from shade into the light and from a slight blur to sharp, so the reveal has the weight of a peel. A cloud of pink and grey smoke hangs over G, H and T and drifts right. The disciplines smear away just ahead of the edge, each letter blurring and drifting down and right, over THOUGHT and cream, the bottom row lingering as a ghost.
- **The bloom.** Just before THOUGHT comes through (7.88 s), an unstable nucleus of paint goes off in the middle of the page, behind the type: a small hot blob that wobbles and explodes outward into a glowing, cell-like membrane, electric blue to the left and magenta to the right, almost to the screen's edges, translucent where it thins, and fades by about 8.6 s. It is the kinetic film's burst (`intro-bloom`: 1.6–3 s of the film over black at twice speed, warped by a boiling turbulence so it never reads as a shape, blurred outward while it explodes, recoloured blue on its left, with a glow, the frame's edges fallen off to black), screened onto the ground and slaved to the intro's clock; the element starts at a quarter size, dead centre under the second line, and grows fast.
- **THOUGHT keeps its colours.** The window lightens onto whatever is behind it, so a bright bloom behind THOUGHT would wash out its pigment. A copy of THOUGHT in black (`nda-intro__shade`), masked and unfolded with it, sits under the window, so the letters show their own colours over the bloom.
- **The ribbon.** From 5.6 to 6.7 s, exactly as the film has it, because it is the film's own: a twisting satin ribbon of paint, blue on one face and magenta on the other, with white catches of light. A thin arc appears across the top of SPIRITUALITY and its left end swells into a blue curl; then an S sweeps up from the left through the rows and folds over on itself above them, billows to the right into a broad, dark fold, and shrinks to a blade that is gone as the rows turn into ETHICS · DESIGN. It is lifted out of the intro concept film frame by frame (`intro-ribbon.webp`: 27 frames, each cut into its pieces and packed) and played on the score's clock at the film's 24 frames a second, placed by the film's first line, so it lands where the film has it. Where the film shows it in front of the letters, it is drawn over ours; where the film shows its paint inside its letters (the foot of SOCIAL, a leaf in an N, the tops of THE), it is drawn inside the window in `darken`, so it shows only inside our letters. The rows it crosses ripple.
- **The sound.** The concept film's own soundtrack (`intro-sound`), which its cuts follow, so it lands on our moments too: the first push, the burst, the last scroll, the tug, and a low boom as THOUGHT comes in. Its ending, which the film cuts off while the last hit is still ringing, is let ring out (a tail grown from its last moments, on the decay it already has), so it trails off under the homepage, about a second after the sweep. It starts with the score (after the still), a frame behind it as the film runs, and stays in step with the clock. Browsers only play sound unasked once the visitor has been active on the site (pressing play on the radio, for one), so the intro tries; where it may not, a Sound on button beside Skip intro starts it from wherever the score is (and Sound off stops it). While it plays, Neta DAO Radio, if it is on, is lowered to a fifth under it (`NetaDAORadio.duck`) and brought back up when it ends, is turned off, or the intro is skipped (which fades it in 0.45 s).
- **The exit.** After a hold, an almost-hairline white bar (2px, a faint glow on the film ahead of it) sweeps left to right in 0.9 s (`cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`), and the page shows behind it. Skip intro and Escape use the same bar, in 0.56 s.

- Every move is a Web Animation on one clock, from a seeded random, so every run is the same film; the letters' film is slaved to that clock. `NDA.intro()` returns a state with `seek(ms)`, `pause()`, `play()` and `skip()` for review.
- THOUGHT's edge is two linear masks intersected, animated through registered custom properties (`--nda-rx`, `--nda-ry`); where those aren't supported, its letters come up one by one in the same order instead.
- Optional: a filmed burst can sit behind the rows instead (`video.nda-intro__bg` in the stage; `data-lead` ms before the burst it starts, `data-level` its strength, `data-fragments` to keep the code-drawn fragments over it). The system ships without one.
- Skip intro (top right), Escape, or leaving the tab end it. It plays once per browser (`localStorage` key `academy-intro-seen-v6`).
- Under reduced motion it never appears; the homepage hero already says the same thing.

## The homepage's arrivals

- **The tag line.** Under the statement the tags fade up into place one after another (4px rise, 420ms, 380ms apart), "For a more open tomorrow" a beat later (700ms), and all of them stay. Once, when the line is on screen; on a first visit, after the intro's bar has gone. `NDA.sequence()`.
- **The wordmark docks.** The header starts without its wordmark; when the statement's first line scrolls under the header the wordmark fades and rises into place (320ms), and leaves again (200ms) if you scroll back up. `NDA.dock()`.
- **Cinema's header** tucks up out of the way while you scroll down and slides back (240ms) as soon as you scroll up. `NDA.autohide()`.
- Under reduced motion the tags are simply there and the wordmark and header switch without moving.

## Small transitions

- CircleLink fill, text-link colour, button background, radio circle: `dur-fast`, `ease`.
- Header menu: opens and closes without animation.
- No parallax, no scroll-triggered reveals of content, no looping decoration besides THOUGHT.
