# ForkMark

Fork's wordmark: FORK, cut. At rest it is the whole word; hover cuts it along a pink-to-blue slash and the lower half slips a step down and right. It stands for the journal wherever the journal is named as a brand.

## Use
- The Fork ProgramPanel's title ("[mark] The Journal"), the JournalPanel column and feature, and links to fork.netadao.org that need a mark. Not inside running text: there, write "Fork".
- Live inline SVG in `currentColor`, so it is `ink` on Paper and cream on Night.
- The cut: hovering or focusing the mark, or the panel or link that holds it, draws the slash left to right (pink to blue, 340ms, fast then easing out), and 70ms in the lower half slips 6 units right and 5 down with a slight overshoot (460ms), opening the gap the slash shows through. Leaving closes the gap (300ms) and fades the slash (200ms). The preview pins the cut on the second mark (`is-split`).
- Touch screens can't hover, so there the mark cuts itself once when three quarters of it is in view (`NDA.forkMark()` adds `is-split`) and stays cut.
- Reduced motion: no drawing or slipping; the mark switches between whole and cut.
- Give it a width; it scales from its 240×65 box. Keep it at least 96px wide. The box doesn't change size when the mark is cut: the lower half slips into space the box already holds.
- The slash colours (`#C9338A` to `#5B8EF0`) belong to the mark. They are not tokens; don't use them anywhere else.
- `NDA.forkMark()` (run by `NDA.enhance()`) gives each inline mark its own gradient id, so marks stay painted when another one on the page is hidden.
- Where CSS or inline SVG can't go (email, social cards), use the cut mark: `assets/Logos/fork-wordmark-ink.svg` on light grounds and `fork-wordmark-paper.svg` on dark ones.

## Don't
- Don't recolour the slash, show the slash without the cut (or the cut without the slash), set FORK in type beside the mark, or fill it with the pigment film.
