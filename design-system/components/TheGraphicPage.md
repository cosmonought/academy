# TheGraphicPage (draft)

`/SexMonstersSuperheroes/`, Sex, Monsters, and Superheroes: On the Graphic. A seminar with its own visual language: it keeps the site's shell (header, facts, fields and buttons, the footer) and brings its comic idiom inside it.

- **Panels.** Its unit is the comic panel: a 2px ink frame with a hard 8px drop, on 20px gutters. Night panels invert. The syllabus is a comic page: Session 0 (Orientation: Between the Visible and the Invisible) as a strip across the top, then twelve panels in rows of differing splits (6/6, 5/7, 5/7, 7/5, 5/7, 7/5). Each panel is headed by its number on the ink chip and its title in the display face, over a two-cell table: the primary works set big in the display face beside the theoretical text. A theoretical text not chosen yet is a dashed To come slot.
- **Captions.** The three description captions stay staggered as panels on a page, one wide on the left, one lower on the right, one under the first, so the eye goes left, across to the right, then back left, as experimental comics sometimes ask. They are numbered 1, 2, 3 on small ink tags at their top-left corners, the way panels sometimes are.
- **Colour.** The session panels are printed in eight comic process colours, each a pale tint under Ben-Day dots of its full colour: yellow, cyan, green, orange, red (a vermilion), purple, brown, and charcoal, the one dark tint (its dots lighter, its type and cells light). No panel sits beside or under one of its own colour. Every panel keeps its dots, so a flat colour never reads as a state. The works sit on Paper inside each panel. Magenta is held back: pink is this page's neon, and it marks the current session, as everywhere on the site; sessions already held print in black on Paper with grey dots (see TheGraphicSyllabus).
- **The cover** is a panel: the copy on the left, the art on the right (the comic covers from the seminar's title card). The title mixes faces like a comic cover: Sex, as the poster's neon sign, in Satisfy (a pale tube in a pink glow, flickering on once as the page opens); MONSTERS, reversed out of a red block, slightly askew; and in spaced mono; Superheroes in NDA Display Heavy. The red belongs to the cover.
- **Lil D** peeks over the top edge of the format panel from the free corner beside the last caption, part of the description rather than a row of his own: up to his eyes, back down a little, then up to standing (never past it, so the cut at the bottom of his picture stays below the panel's edge); a hand-drawn arrow draws toward him and his name is written at its other end, stroke by stroke. It plays once the captions and his corner are all in full view (on a screen too short for that, once his corner is) and holds when done. Click him to replay. Reduced motion shows everything at rest. His image (`assets/Imagery/lil-d.webp`) is the supplied artwork, its black ground taken out, shown mirrored so he faces his name and the page. He is 195px wide (124px on phones). The arrow starts under the middle of his name and points at the middle of him.
- **State.** Forthcoming, in the blue Next badge; Express interest is the one primary action, and its form sits in a Paper panel inside a Night one.

Below 1024px the captions and panels run full width and Lil D takes a row of his own above the format panel; on phones the cover stacks (art under the copy), each panel's two cells stack, and Lil D is smaller. Draft for review.
