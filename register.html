Guest Sessions

Lead a Session

Neta DAO Academy welcomes proposals from scholars, practitioners, artists, and community members who wish to lead a one-time session on a topic within the Academy's scope.

Guest sessions typically last 60–90 minutes and can take any format: lecture, seminar, workshop, screening, or something else. The only requirement is that the topic connects to Web3, the Interchain, or the intellectual traditions we engage (philosophy, psychoanalysis, political theory, art, etc.).

To propose a session, complete the registration form above and describe your proposal in the background field. We'll be in touch.
